**Name** : Hello English! – Kids English

**Short description** : Learn English through play, from preschool to age 15, following the curriculum.

**Full description** :
Hello English! helps children discover and improve their English from preschool to the end of middle school, following the official French national curriculum. Free, with no ads and no account.

Note: the app's instructions are in French; the words and sentences to learn are in English.

🎈 12 LEVELS, FROM PRESCHOOL TO MIDDLE SCHOOL
• Preschool (ages 3 to 5): first contact with English, all spoken, with pictures: colours, numbers, animals, family, body, feelings
• Ages 6 to 8: greetings, classroom, home, days, months, weather, first sentences, festivals
• Ages 9 to 10: daily routine, telling the time, describing people, jobs, directions, English-speaking countries
• Ages 11 to 15: the cultural themes of the 2025 French curriculum (the UK, Ireland, the USA…) and middle-school grammar (present, past simple, present perfect, comparatives, passive, conditionals…)

🎮 8 GAMES TO LEARN
• Discover: tap a picture to hear the word
• Listen: hear a word and find the right picture
• Speak: repeat into the microphone, the app listens and gives a result
• Right word: match the picture with the English word
• Memory: find the pairs
• Spell: put the letters in order
• Quiz: grammar and culture, with an explanation after each answer
• Sentences: put the words in order

🎤 SPEAKING ENGLISH
In every unit, pupils hear a word or sentence in English, repeat it, and the app tells them whether it understood: "Bravo!", "Almost!" or "Try again".

📈 PROGRESS TRACKING
• Stars to earn in every game
• "Continue" opens the next activity straight away
• Progress for each level and for each skill: understanding, speaking, writing, memory, grammar and culture

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS AND TEACHERS
• Content aligned with the official curriculum: CEFR levels (A1 to B1 by age 15), themes of each school stage, cultural topics of middle school
• No ads, no account, no personal data collected
• Progress stays on the device
• Games work offline, except "Speak", which uses the phone's speech recognition
